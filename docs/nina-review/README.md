# Reporting, for Nina

The reporting tool is built. **None of it is switched on** — nothing in here is in front of a client, and nothing will be until you've looked.

**Updated 28 October, after your review.** All thirty-eight decisions now carry your answer, and the ten you changed are built. The screens below have been retaken, so what you are looking at is what the changes actually produced rather than what they were before.

Read in this order:

### 1. [What was decided for you](01-decisions.md)

Thirty-eight questions that came up while this was being built and are really yours. Rather than stop each time, Dom picked an answer and wrote it down. Each one says what was chosen, why, what changes if you'd rather it went the other way — and now, at the end of each, **what you said on 28 October**.

**This is the important one.** Twenty-eight you agreed; ten you changed, and those ten are built. Your question about up-and-down arrows is answered at the foot of that page.

### 2. [Every screen, as it looks](02-screens.md)

Twenty-eight screens in `screens/`, each one on a laptop and on a phone — Launches, members reporting on themselves, and uploading a file. The table says what each screen is and which decision it's an example of.

The screens from the earlier work — the monthly report itself, targets, benchmarks, what a client sees — are in `screens-stage-3/`, fifteen of them, also at both widths.

**The changes from your review are in `screens-review/`**, nine of them at both widths: the Focus area tag, the hours figure with no colour on it, the month-being-prepared screen, the launch figures that moved, the Send update button, the planner's first guess, and a member's own upload card.

### 3. [Before any of this goes on](03-launch-checklist.md)

Ten things that can only be done with a real client, a real month, or your eyes. Including one that was skipped and shouldn't have been, and two your review added.

---

**A note on the figures.** Everything you'll see is test data: a fake retainer client called Northwind Studio and a fake member called Ruth. The numbers are chosen to make each screen show what it does — a launch that hit its goal, a month with a figure missing, a file for the wrong month. None of it is anybody's real business.
