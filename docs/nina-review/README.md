## Every sentence that is yours

These are written down in one place and nowhere else, so changing one changes it everywhere. All of them are already in your words; this is to check we typed them as you meant them.

**The two panel headings**
- Look at these first 👀
- What went WELL this month

**When a figure moves, and higher is better**
- `{number} MORE {thing} than last month - stunning`
- `{number} fewer {thing} than last month - worth a proper look 👀`
- `{Figure} up from {old} to {new} & that's no accident`
- `{Figure} dropped from {old} to {new}... let's work out WHY`

**When a figure moves, and lower is better** — churn, cost per lead, clients who left
- `{Figure} down {amount} - exactly the direction we want`
- `{Figure} up {amount} - not the direction we want, let's dig into WHY`

**Against a target**
- `{Figure} beat your target by {percent} WOOOO`
- `{Figure} is at {percent} of your target - not there YET`

**The traffic lights** — each one always carries a word as well as a colour, and says what it's measured against: *On track vs. target*, *Close vs. benchmark*, *Off track vs. last month*.

**The two warnings** — the publishing one and the taking-it-back one, both quoted above.

## Things we decided for you — please confirm

**1. The two sentences for figures where falling is good.**
Churn, cost per lead and clients who left were coming out backwards: two fewer clients leaving was being called "2 MORE clients who left - stunning". You gave us the two sentences above on 6 October and we've used them. Please check they read right to you in place.

**2. Targets appear in a fixed order, not "worst first".**
The Overview shows up to five target bars in the system's own order. We could have put the furthest-behind one at the top instead. We didn't, so that the same five stay in the same place each month and you can find one with your eye rather than reading. Say if you'd rather see the worst first.

**3. Two figures that are really one piece of news now show one sentence.**
"Issues raised up 3" and "Issues per 10 clients up 1.0" are the same event told twice, so only the plain count appears. But if two figures are genuinely telling different stories — more new clients while the close rate drops — both still appear. Please confirm that's the behaviour you want.

**4. Benchmarks are per client, not per funnel or per campaign.**
A benchmark is set once for a figure, for the whole business. There is no separate benchmark for each funnel or each ad campaign. Confirm that's right, or tell us where you'd want one.

**5. A figure nobody can work out shows a dash, never a guess.**
If a number is missing, anything built on it shows "—" rather than a number calculated as if the missing one were zero. It is honest, but it does mean a month where nobody typed "0" shows a dash instead of a number.

**6. The primary buttons are orange with dark text, not white text.**
White on our orange isn't readable enough to meet the accessibility standard. This is the one place the screens differ from the reference poster, and it was a readability decision rather than a design preference.

**7. Only the campaign with no goal set is flagged in the Goal column.**
On the Ads table, campaigns with a goal leave that column empty and only the unset one says "Not set". Confirm you'd rather see the problem flagged than every goal listed.
