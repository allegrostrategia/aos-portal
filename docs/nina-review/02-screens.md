# Every screen, as it looks

Twenty-eight screens, each one twice: `-desktop.png` and `-phone.png`. They're in `screens/`, numbered in the order somebody meets them.

Everything here is **switched off on the live site.** None of it is in front of a client. The figures are test data on a fake client called Northwind Studio and a fake member called Ruth.

---

## Launches (01–11)

| | What it is | What's yours to approve |
